Can Reciprocal Learning Improve Math Achievement and Reduce Math Anxiety? ↓ A child can arrive at the correct answer and still struggle to explain why the method works. For a teacher or parent, that raises a more useful question than whether the answer is right or wrong: has the child understood the idea, or learned to repeat a procedure?
