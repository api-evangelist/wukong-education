Official paper certificates for the 2026 Math League International Summer Challenge have arrived at WuKong Education and are now being prepared for distribution to participating students and families who requested physical copies. The arrival of the printed certificates marks one of the final post-competition steps for students who took part in this year’s Summer Challenge. According to the official 2026 Math League International Summer Challenge announcement, the online competition was held on August 16, 2026, with seven divisions covering Grade 3, Grade 4, Grade 5, Grade 6, Grade 7, Grades 8
