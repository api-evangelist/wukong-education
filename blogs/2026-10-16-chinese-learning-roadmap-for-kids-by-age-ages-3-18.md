A Chinese learning roadmap for kids can help parents understand what to learn next at different stages. From listening and speaking to pinyin, Chinese characters, reading, and writing, each stage has different priorities. Age is a useful starting point, but a child’s current Chinese level, home language, and learning goals also matter.
