A qualified Chinese teacher for kids is not simply someone who speaks Chinese. Parents can judge any teacher with four checks: Teach (explains, corrects, gets the child speaking), Match (fits the child’s age, level, and home-language background), Show (visible in a trial class), and Track (explains progress and next steps).
