How to restart Chinese learning after a break: first understand why your child stopped, then move through three stages: warm up, rebuild, and return. Children who have learned Chinese before may still recognize familiar words, songs, stories, or characters, so restarting may be easier than beginning from scratch. Rather than picking up exactly where your child left off, start with familiar, low-pressure activities and adjust to what your child can comfortably do now.
