Comparing Chinese class prices and packages for kids is easiest when you calculate the cost of the lessons your child is actually likely to complete, rather than comparing package prices alone. Parents should compare lesson length, class format, package size, expiration rules, and extra fees such as materials. A lower sticker price is not automatically a better value.
