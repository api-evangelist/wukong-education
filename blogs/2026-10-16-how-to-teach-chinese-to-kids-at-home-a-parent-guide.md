Teaching Chinese to kids at home works best when parents create regular chances to hear and use Chinese, build a habit, and make language learning feel safe. Try four steps: Anchor a routine moment, Input Chinese through real interaction, Output by inviting a low-pressure response, and Handoff to a teacher or course when a child needs structured help with reading, characters, or extended expression. Parents do not need to replace a language teacher; they can make Chinese part of family life and notice when home practice needs support.
