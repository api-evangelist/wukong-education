A child who can read Chinese characters but can’t write them is showing a common gap between recognition and recall. Parents can support writing with four steps: See familiar characters, Trace their shapes, Build them from familiar parts, and Recall them from memory. Children do not need to write every character they can read; they can begin with a few useful ones and build confidence as they go.
