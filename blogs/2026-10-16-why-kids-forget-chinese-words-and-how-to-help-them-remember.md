Kids forget Chinese words when they have seen or reread them but have not had enough chances to retrieve them from memory. Recognizing a word on a flashcard is easier than recalling it without a prompt, saying it clearly, and using it in a sentence. Short, spaced Chinese vocabulary practice gives a child repeated chances to do those harder steps.
