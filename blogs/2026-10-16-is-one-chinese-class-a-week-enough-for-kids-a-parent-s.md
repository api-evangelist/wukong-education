One Chinese class a week can be enough for kids, depending on their goals, starting level, age, and opportunities to encounter Chinese between lessons. For children learning for fun or maintaining existing skills, one class may work well with some regular exposure at home. Children who want to start speaking or develop reading and writing skills from scratch may need more support, especially when no one speaks Chinese at home.
