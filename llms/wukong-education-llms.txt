# WuKong Education

> WuKong Education (wukongsch.com) is an online education company headquartered in Auckland, New Zealand, offering live online classes in Chinese language, math, and English ELA for kids aged roughly 3-18, taught by certified teachers to students across 100+ countries and regions. It is a consumer edtech business: it publishes no public developer portal, API documentation, or machine-readable API specifications.
